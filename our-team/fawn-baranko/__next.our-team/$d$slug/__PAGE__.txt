With a degree in Interior Design and more than a decade of design experience, she brings creativity, practical planning, and personal attention to our residential renovation projects. Her approach makes homes more beautiful, functional, and enjoyable to live in.</p>
			<p>Fawn believes the best design does more than look beautiful—it makes life better. Whether refreshing a single room, reimagining a kitchen or bathroom, or guiding the design of a full-home renovation, she considers each homeowner’s vision, routines, and priorities. Working with DMV General Contracting Group, she helps bring these ideas into a cohesive design that balances personal style with everyday functionality and reflects the people who call the space home.</p>
			<p>Her 20 years of service as a Navy nurse bring a foundation of care, organization, and attention to detail to her design work. This experience informs her commitment to creating homes that support clients’ needs today and for years to come. She has a particular passion for designing beautiful, accessible spaces that offer comfort and ease of use for people of all physical abilities.</p>
			<p>Clients value Fawn's ability to listen closely, understand what matters most, and translate their ideas into a clear design direction. Through thoughtful communication and collaboration with homeowners and the DMV General Contracting Group team, she helps connect design decisions with the overall renovation. From the initial conversation through the choices that shape each space, she guides clients with confidence and care, keeping the experience centered on how they want to live.</p>
