💧 Laurel's finished basements — whether in the City of Laurel, West Laurel in MoCo, or Laurel Lakes in PG County — sit on below-grade concrete slabs that transmit ground moisture upward year-round, regardless of how dry the basement appears. Standard carpet padding on a below-grade slab creates conditions for mold growth within 2–5 years in Maryland's climate, producing the musty smell that is one of the most common finished basement carpet complaints across all three Laurel jurisdictions. DMV General Contracting Group installs moisture-barrier padding on every Laurel finished basement carpet project as standard practice — the sealed foam bottom layer blocks moisture transmission from the concrete slab before it reaches the carpet pad above.
