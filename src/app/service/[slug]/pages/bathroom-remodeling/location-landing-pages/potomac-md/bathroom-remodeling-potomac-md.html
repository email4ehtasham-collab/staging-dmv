Why Waterproofing Is the Most Important Step in Your Potomac Bathroom

The single most important quality indicator in a bathroom renovation is something you'll never see after the project is complete — the waterproofing. A properly waterproofed shower will last 20–30+ years without moisture issues. A poorly waterproofed shower begins failing within 3–7 years, with water penetrating behind tiles, damaging subfloor and framing, and eventually requiring a full gut renovation to correct.

DMV-GC Group applies a continuous waterproofing membrane system — not just cementboard — to all shower floor pans and walls. We pre-slope shower floors to the drain at the correct pitch (typically 1/4" per foot). We waterproof all curbs, niches, and penetrations. We perform a flood test on completed shower pans before tile is installed to confirm waterproofing integrity.

⚠️ What to Watch For: The most common sign of a poorly waterproofed shower is grout cracking and tile loosening within 2–5 years of installation — caused by water getting behind the tile and expanding the substrate. If you've seen this in a previous bathroom renovation, it was almost certainly a waterproofing failure, not a tile adhesive failure. DMV-GC Group's waterproofing approach is designed specifically to prevent this.
