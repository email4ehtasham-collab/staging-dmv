Bathroom Remodeling for Garrett Park's Historic Housing Stock

More than anything else, Garrett Park's residential character is defined by its historic homes — 1890s–1900s frame Victorians from the town's founding as a planned railroad suburb, the distinctive 1920s–30s Spanish Bungalows, and the 1950s ranches and split-levels of adjacent Garrett Park Estates, all set among the protected trees of a registered arboretum. Understanding how these older homes were originally plumbed, wired, and framed — and how to renovate them sensitively — is essential to a successful bathroom project, and it's where DMV-GC Group's experience makes a real difference.

🛁 What Makes Garrett Park Bathrooms Different

  • Bathrooms added to older floor plans — many Victorians were built before indoor plumbing, so original baths are often retrofitted into awkward or undersized spaces that benefit from a smart redesign
  • Few bathrooms — older homes frequently have just one or two baths, so adding an ensuite, powder room, or basement bath is one of the highest-return upgrades available
  • Aging plumbing & wiring — galvanized supply lines, cast-iron drain stacks, and outdated electrical are common in homes this age and often need updating during a remodel
  • Plaster walls & older subfloors — period construction calls for careful demolition and the right substrate prep so new tile and waterproofing last
  • Period-sensitive finishes — many owners want to modernize systems while preserving the home's character, balancing classic style with today's performance
  • Arboretum & historic considerations — Garrett Park's tree-protection ordinance and historic-district review apply mainly to exterior work, which we account for from the start

Tub-to-Shower Conversions, Walk-In Showers & Added Bathrooms

For Garrett Park homeowners whose bathrooms feel dated or cramped, three projects deliver the biggest impact: converting an unused tub into a modern walk-in shower, upgrading to an accessible aging-in-place layout, or adding a bathroom to a home that was built with only one or two.

Tub-to-shower conversions are popular both in the town's older Victorian and bungalow homes and in the mid-century homes of Garrett Park Estates. We remove the existing tub, rebuild the wet area with a proper bonded waterproofing system, and install a custom tile or low-maintenance walk-in shower — with frameless glass, niches, and bench seating — designed to suit the character of the home.

Adding a bathroom — whether a basement bath, a primary-suite ensuite, or a main-level powder room — requires careful planning of plumbing rough-in, drainage, and ventilation, especially in older construction. DMV-GC Group's team assesses how your home is built before designing the most efficient way to run new lines, then manages the full Montgomery County DPS and WSSC permit process.
