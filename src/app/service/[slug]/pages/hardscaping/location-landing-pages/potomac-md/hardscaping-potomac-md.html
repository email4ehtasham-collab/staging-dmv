Frequently Asked Questions — Hardscaping in Potomac, MD

Does hardscaping in Potomac require a permit?

Simple ground-level patios and walkways typically do not require a permit in unincorporated Montgomery County. Retaining walls over 30 inches, outdoor kitchens with gas or electrical, and driveways connecting to county roads require MoCo DPS permits filed at 2425 Reedie Drive, Wheaton. Walls over 4 feet require engineering review. Avenel HOA ARC approval is required for significant exterior modifications before permits are filed. DMV General Contracting Group confirms all requirements at the free consultation.

Does Potomac's R-200 zoning restrict where I can build a patio or retaining wall?

Ground-level patios are generally not subject to R-200 setback requirements. Structures on or adjacent to the hardscape — roofed outdoor kitchens, pavilions, and pergolas — are typically treated as accessory structures with setback requirements from property lines. Retaining walls may also be subject to setback requirements depending on height and location. DMV General Contracting Group reviews R-200 requirements for every Potomac hardscape scope that includes a structure before the design is finalized.

What is the best pool surround material for a Potomac estate property?

Tumbled travertine is the most popular choice for Potomac pool surrounds — its porous surface provides natural slip resistance when wet, it stays cooler underfoot than darker stone in summer heat, and its warm tones complement formal pool environments on estate properties. Brushed bluestone and large-format porcelain are also used for Potomac pools. DMV General Contracting Group coordinates pool surround material selection and installation with your pool contractor's schedule.

Does my Avenel property need HOA approval for hardscaping?

Yes — Avenel's HOA Architectural Review Committee typically requires ARC approval for significant exterior hardscape additions including large patio installations, retaining wall systems, driveway changes, and outdoor kitchen structures. DMV General Contracting Group confirms Avenel HOA requirements, prepares ARC documentation and submits on your behalf, and coordinates the ARC and MoCo DPS permit processes concurrently to minimize project timeline impact.
