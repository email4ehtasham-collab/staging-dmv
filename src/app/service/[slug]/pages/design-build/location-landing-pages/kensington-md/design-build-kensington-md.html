The Town of Kensington Permit Process — What Every Contractor Must Know

📋 Town of Kensington Permits — 3710 Mitchell Street, Not MoCo DPS

  • The Town of Kensington issues its own building permits: Kensington is an incorporated town with its own municipal government. For properties within the incorporated Town limits, all building permits — for additions, structural work, ADU construction, significant kitchen and bathroom renovations, electrical upgrades, plumbing modifications, and new construction — must be filed with the Town of Kensington permit office at 3710 Mitchell Street, Kensington, MD 20895. Montgomery County DPS at 2425 Reedie Drive, Wheaton has no jurisdiction over permits for properties within Kensington Town limits.
  • Why this matters for your design-build project: Contractors who are unaware of this distinction file with MoCo DPS, receive a rejection or a routing to the correct authority, and lose weeks or months of permit timeline. On a Kensington home addition or ADU project with a 6–8 week permit review timeline, that error can push a project's construction start from spring into fall. DMV-GC Group's in-house permit team has filed with the Town of Kensington permit office and understands the Town's review process, inspection schedule, and documentation requirements.
  • Flooring-only and cosmetic projects typically do not require a Town permit: Pure flooring replacement, painting, cabinet refacing, and similar cosmetic work generally falls below the permit threshold in Kensington. When those same projects are part of a broader scope — a kitchen gut remodel, a bathroom structural reconfiguration, or a whole-home renovation — the broader scope triggers the permit requirement at the Town office. DMV-GC Group assesses permit requirements for every Kensington project during the free consultation and advises honestly on what requires Town review and what does not.
  • The MoCo DPS exception — Kensington postal code outside Town limits: A small number of addresses with Kensington ZIP codes (20895) fall outside the incorporated Town limits in unincorporated Montgomery County. For these properties, MoCo DPS is the correct permit authority. DMV-GC Group confirms jurisdiction during the consultation — Town or County — before any permit-required scope begins.
  • Kensington's zoning — what it allows for additions and ADUs: The Town of Kensington's residential zoning governs lot coverage, setbacks, building height, and ADU eligibility for properties within Town limits. Our architects review these parameters during the free consultation, assess the maximum buildable envelope on your specific lot, and design additions and ADUs that maximize livable space within the Town's zoning constraints. We do not design additions that look good on paper but cannot be permitted — every design decision is made with the Town's zoning requirements already factored in.
