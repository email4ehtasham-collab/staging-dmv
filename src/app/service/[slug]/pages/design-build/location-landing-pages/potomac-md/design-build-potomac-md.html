What Design-Build Means — And Why It Matters for Potomac

The design-build method is a project delivery approach where a single firm is responsible for both the design and construction of your renovation. This is fundamentally different from the traditional approach where a homeowner hires an architect separately, receives finished plans, bids those plans out to general contractors, and then manages two separate firms across the life of the project.

🏗️ The core difference for Potomac homeowners: In the traditional model, the architect designs without knowing exactly what the GC will charge to build it — so plans come back over budget, redesign adds months, and the homeowner mediates every conflict between two firms who have different financial incentives. In the design-build model, the same team that designs also builds — so every design decision is made with full knowledge of construction cost, schedule impact, and buildability. One contract. One responsible party. No gap to fall through.
