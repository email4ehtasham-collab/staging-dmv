Siding Types We Install in Potomac, MD

Signs You Need New Siding on Your Potomac Home

🔍

Cracking & Warping

Visible cracks, warps, or buckled panels — moisture and air infiltration have compromised the barrier.

🍄

Rot & Fungal Growth

Soft spots, mold, mildew, or dark staining — especially at the bottom of panels and around window frames.

🎨

Frequent Paint Failure

Paint peeling or bubbling within a few years of repainting — failing siding substrate is often the root cause.

💧

Interior Water Damage

Water stains on interior walls or in the attic beneath exterior siding — a serious sign of active infiltration.

🌡️

Rising Energy Bills

Failing siding compromises the thermal envelope — drafts drive up heating and cooling costs significantly on estate-scale homes.

🏚️

Faded or Dated Appearance

Severely faded or outdated siding reduces Potomac curb appeal and impacts resale value in a market where exterior presentation matters greatly.

New Siding & Energy Efficiency for Potomac Estate Homes

One of the most significant — and often overlooked — benefits of new siding on a Potomac estate is the improvement in home energy efficiency. At 5,000–10,000 square feet, the exterior wall area of a Potomac home is substantial, and the energy impact of air infiltration through failing siding is proportionally large:

🌦️

House Wrap & Moisture Barrier

Every DMV General Contracting Group Potomac siding project includes installation of a continuous, properly lapped weather-resistant barrier beneath the new siding — with all penetrations individually flashed and sealed. This dramatically reduces air infiltration and moisture intrusion — the two biggest energy efficiency and durability factors in older Potomac estate homes.

🧱

Rigid Foam Under Fiber Cement

For James Hardie installations on Potomac estates, we can add a continuous layer of rigid foam insulation board beneath the fiber cement panels — improving thermal performance without interior wall work. On a 6,000+ sq ft Potomac exterior, a continuous R-3 to R-5 foam layer makes a meaningful difference in heating and cooling costs and improves comfort throughout the home.

🔌

Insulated Vinyl Option

For Potomac homeowners selecting vinyl siding, insulated vinyl adds a continuous foam backer that improves R-value and reduces exterior wall noise transmission — a benefit particularly relevant for Potomac estates near MacArthur Boulevard or other road corridors. Insulated vinyl also improves panel rigidity, reducing the hollow sound and slight flexibility of standard vinyl on large wall expanses.

🔒

Sealing Penetrations & Transitions

Proper flashing, caulking, and sealing around all windows, doors, utility penetrations, hose bibs, and exterior lighting fixtures is as important as the siding itself on a Potomac estate. Our installers detail every penetration correctly — eliminating the air leakage at transition points that poor siding installations routinely leave behind and that become water infiltration points within 2–5 years.
