Common Roofing Problems We Address

Roof Leaks

Leaks around shingles, flashing, penetrations or roof transitions can allow moisture into the attic and interior. Identifying the source is the first step toward an appropriate repair.

Storm Damage

Wind and severe weather can damage roofing materials and expose vulnerable areas. A post-storm inspection can help document visible issues and determine next steps.

Aging Roofing

Older roofs may develop recurring repairs, worn materials and reduced weather resistance. An assessment can help determine whether maintenance, repair or replacement makes sense.

Flashing & Detail Issues

Roof-to-wall transitions, penetrations and other details require careful attention because failures in these areas can lead to localized water intrusion.

Roof Repair vs. Roof Replacement

The right solution depends on the age and condition of the roofing system, the extent of the damage and the likelihood of recurring problems. We evaluate the existing roof before recommending the scope of work.

SituationPotential Approach
Small, localized damageTargeted repair may be appropriate when the surrounding roofing remains in good condition.
Recurring leaks or widespread deteriorationA broader repair or replacement evaluation may be warranted.
Severe storm damageInspection and immediate protection can be followed by the required permanent repair or replacement work.
Older roof nearing the end of its service lifeReplacement may provide a more dependable long-term solution than repeated repairs.
