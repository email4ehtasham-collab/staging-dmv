Roof Repair vs. Roof Replacement in North Bethesda, MD

Roof Repair

Repair can make sense when damage is localized, such as a limited leak, damaged shingles or a flashing issue, and the overall roofing system remains serviceable.

Roof Replacement

Replacement may be more practical when the roof is aging, deterioration is widespread, recurring leaks are present or the roofing system can no longer provide reliable protection.

Professional Inspection

An inspection helps identify the source and extent of the problem so homeowners can make a more informed repair-versus-replacement decision.
