Patio Materials for Wheaton Homes

🧱 Concrete Pavers

Most popular for Wheaton's mid-century housing stock — durable, repairable, and available in earth-tone profiles that complement 1950s–1970s architecture. Individual units replaceable if damaged. Best value choice for Wheaton homeowners balancing quality and budget.

🪨 Bluestone (Uniform Cut)

Uniform-cut Pennsylvania bluestone in running bond or stacked pattern — a step up in quality and character. Cool gray tone and clean edges work well with Wheaton's traditional colonial and cape cod profiles. Mortar-set for long-term stability.

🪨 Irregular Flagstone

Natural irregular stone in an informal layout — suited to Wheaton's more garden-oriented backyard settings. Wide joints planted with ground cover integrate naturally with established garden beds and mature landscaping.

🏗️ Stamped Concrete

Cost-effective for larger Wheaton patio areas. Custom patterns in earth tones. Properly sealed for Maryland's climate. Best for homeowners on tighter budgets where the patio area is large and natural stone or unit pavers would be cost-prohibitive at scale.

🪨 Travertine

Warm cream and tan tones — works well for Wheaton's transitional and contemporary homes and for outdoor kitchen areas. Tumbled travertine for informal settings. Requires sealing in outdoor kitchen and food-service areas.

🧱 Large-Format Porcelain

Contemporary choice for Wheaton's newer and renovated properties — non-porous, stain-resistant, and available in stone-look profiles. Frost-rated porcelain required for Maryland outdoor installation. Practical for outdoor kitchen countertop areas.
