Patio Materials for Bethesda Homes

Bethesda's upscale housing stock — colonials, contemporaries, and larger traditional homes — calls for patio materials that match the quality and architectural character of the home. DMV General Contracting Group discusses material options at the free consultation and recommends based on the home's style, the intended use, and the project budget.

🪨 Natural Bluestone

The most popular choice for Bethesda's traditional colonials — cool gray tone, natural texture, timeless appearance. Properly set and sealed for Maryland's freeze-thaw cycles. Available in irregular (natural cleft) and uniform (thermaled or sawn) finishes.

🪨 Travertine

Warm tan and cream tones with natural variation — a popular choice for contemporary Bethesda homes and those with pool areas. Tumbled travertine has a natural, aged appearance; honed travertine offers a cleaner, more modern profile.

🪨 Flagstone

Natural irregular stone set in sand or mortar — a classic landscape aesthetic that works well with Bethesda's mature tree canopy and established garden settings. Wide variety of stone types and color ranges available.

🧱 Concrete Pavers

Durable, repairable, and available in a wide range of profiles, textures, and colors. Individual pavers can be replaced if damaged — an advantage over poured concrete. A practical choice for larger Bethesda patio areas and driveways.

🏗️ Stamped Concrete

Poured concrete stamped with custom patterns and integrally colored — a cost-effective option for large patio areas and pool surrounds. Requires proper sealing and occasional resealing in Maryland's climate to prevent surface wear.

🪟 Porcelain Tile

Large-format porcelain pavers — 24"×24" and larger — deliver a contemporary, refined look for covered outdoor areas, outdoor kitchens, and screened porch floors. Frost-rated porcelain required for outdoor installation in Maryland.

Design & Material Selection

Permit Confirmation & Filing

6

Final Review & Walkthrough

Completed patio inspected and reviewed with you. Drainage tested, all surfaces cleaned, and any required sealing applied. Project documentation and warranty information provided at project close.
