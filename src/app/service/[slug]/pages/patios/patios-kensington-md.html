Patio Materials That Suit Kensington's Character

Kensington's pre-1950 housing stock has a distinct architectural character — craftsman bungalows, American foursquares, and traditional colonials with mature landscaping and established garden settings. The right patio material reads as a natural extension of these homes; the wrong material reads as an addition that doesn't belong. DMV General Contracting Group discusses material options at the free consultation with Kensington's architectural character as the starting point.

🪨 Irregular Flagstone

Natural irregular stone set in sand or mortar — the most organic-looking and period-appropriate choice for Kensington's craftsman and cottage-style homes. Wide joints filled with moss or creeping thyme integrate naturally into established garden settings. Available in bluestone, Pennsylvania flagstone, and quartzite.

🪨 Pennsylvania Bluestone

Cut in uniform rectangles or irregular shapes — bluestone's cool gray tone and natural texture complement Kensington's traditional colonials and foursquares. More formal than irregular flagstone; appropriate for homes with more structured garden layouts.

🧱 Reclaimed or New Brick

Running bond, herringbone, or basket weave patterns — brick is the most period-authentic choice for Kensington's older colonials and foursquares with brick details. Reclaimed brick from the same era as the home delivers the most authentic character.

🧱 Concrete Pavers (Natural Stone Profile)

Pavers in tumbled, aged, or natural stone textures — a durable, repairable alternative to natural stone at lower cost. Best choices for Kensington are earth-tone pavers in smaller unit sizes that read as period-appropriate rather than modern large-format slabs.

🏗️ Stamped Concrete

Stamped patterns and integral color — suitable for Kensington patio areas where budget is the primary driver. Cobblestone and slate patterns in warm earth tones read most naturally with Kensington's older homes. Less repairable than unit pavers if surface damage occurs.

🌿 Permeable Pavers

Permeable paver systems allow rainwater to pass through the patio surface into the soil below — recommended where patio installation is near the drip line of significant mature trees. Protects root zones while delivering an attractive, functional patio surface.

Frequently Asked Questions — Patio Installation in Kensington, MD

Where are patio-related permits filed in Kensington, MD?

With the Town of Kensington at 3710 Mitchell Street, Kensington, MD 20895 — not Montgomery County DPS. Simple ground-level patios typically do not require a permit. Pergolas, outdoor kitchens with gas or electrical connections, and retaining walls require Town of Kensington permits. DMV General Contracting Group confirms requirements at the free consultation and manages all filings in-house.

Can a patio be installed near the mature trees in my Kensington yard?

Yes, with proper planning. Mature tree root zones extend well beyond the drip line — excavation too close to significant roots can permanently damage or kill the tree. DMV General Contracting Group assesses root zone proximity before any patio layout is finalized, adjusts the design to maintain safe setbacks where possible, and recommends permeable paver systems for areas where patio coverage within the root zone is unavoidable.

What patio material is most appropriate for a Kensington bungalow?

Irregular flagstone or Pennsylvania bluestone set in sand or mortar is the most period-appropriate and architecturally compatible choice for Kensington's craftsman bungalows — natural texture, organic appearance, and a look that ages well alongside mature landscaping. Reclaimed brick is also an excellent option for homes with brick exterior details. Concrete pavers in earth-tone, tumbled profiles are a practical lower-cost alternative. Large-format modern concrete slabs are generally not recommended for homes of this era and character.

Does my patio need drainage planning in Kensington?

Yes. Kensington's older lots often have compacted soils and established root systems that affect drainage. A new patio creates an impermeable surface that redirects rainwater — drainage slope must be planned away from the home's foundation. Where mature trees are adjacent, permeable paver systems allow water to continue reaching root zones. DMV General Contracting Group plans drainage as part of every Kensington patio scope.

How much does patio installation cost in Kensington, MD?

Kensington patio costs reflect the community's lot sizes — smaller and more intimate than Potomac estates, larger projects are less common. A concrete paver patio starts in the $7,000–$14,000 range. A natural flagstone or bluestone patio runs $14,000–$28,000+. A pergola or fire pit addition runs $10,000–$25,000+ depending on scope.
