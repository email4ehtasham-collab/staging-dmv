C&O Canal Moisture Expertise — Why It Matters for Your Cabin John, MD Addition

Cabin John's position between the C&O Canal and Cabin John Creek creates the highest ambient moisture conditions in Montgomery County — and home additions here require a contractor who designs for that moisture environment from the start. Crawlspace foundation conditions in many Cabin John homes mean that addition foundations must be designed and waterproofed with Cabin John's elevated ground moisture in mind. Additions that connect to existing crawlspace-foundation Cabin John homes require moisture barrier continuity across the old-to-new foundation transition — a detail that generic addition contractors do not plan for. Additionally, Cabin John's wooded lots create irregular terrain that affects excavation and foundation approaches. DMV General Contracting Group's architects and structural engineers design Cabin John additions with moisture management as a core design criterion — not an afterthought.
