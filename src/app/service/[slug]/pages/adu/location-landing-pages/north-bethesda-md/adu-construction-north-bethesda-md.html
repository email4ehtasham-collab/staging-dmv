Montgomery County ADU Permits for North Bethesda Homeowners

North Bethesda is an unincorporated community in Montgomery County — unlike neighboring Kensington, Garrett Park, or the Chevy Chase villages, there is no separate Town or Village permit authority here. All ADU permits go through Montgomery County DPS. As the contractor headquartered in North Bethesda, DMV-GC Group manages this process from our office minutes from your home.

📋 Montgomery County ADU Requirements — North Bethesda

  • Lot size minimum: 6,000 sq ft — the vast majority of North Bethesda single-family properties qualify. Pike District townhomes on smaller lots should be verified during the consultation.
  • ADU size maximum: 1,200 sq ft or 50% of the main home's gross floor area, whichever is less. For North Bethesda's typical 1,400–2,400 sq ft homes, the 50% rule often applies — a 1,600 sq ft split-level's ADU cannot exceed 800 sq ft.
  • Egress in sleeping areas: Every bedroom in the ADU requires egress window compliance. Split-level above-grade side windows often already satisfy this — below-grade windows typically require enlargement or installation.
  • Separate entrance: Required. Many split-levels already have this. Below-grade basement ADUs without an existing side entrance require a new entrance cut.
  • Owner-occupancy: The property owner must reside in either the main home or the ADU. Pure investment properties without owner-occupancy do not qualify under the current county ordinance.
  • Certificate of Occupancy: Required before the ADU can be legally rented or occupied. DMV-GC Group manages the COO application and all required Montgomery County inspections as a standard part of every North Bethesda ADU project — from our office in the community.
