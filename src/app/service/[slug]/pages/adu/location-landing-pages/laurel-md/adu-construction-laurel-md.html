Laurel sits at the junction of three separate permit authorities — the City of Laurel (incorporated municipality with its own permit office at 8103 Sandy Spring Road), unincorporated Montgomery County (governed by Montgomery County DPS), and unincorporated Prince George's County (governed by PG County DPS). Types of ADUs We Build in Laurel, MD

Laurel's housing stock spans a wide range — from 1960s–80s split-levels and colonials in West Laurel and Montpelier to 1950s–70s ranches and Capes in older City of Laurel neighborhoods to newer townhome construction in Laurel Lakes and Russett. Each presents different ADU opportunities:

Most Practical Across All Laurel

Basement ADU Conversion

Laurel's single-family homes across all three jurisdictions have basements well-suited to ADU conversion. Split-levels in West Laurel and Montpelier offer the same natural light and ceiling height advantages seen in Silver Spring and Wheaton. Full below-grade basements in older City of Laurel colonials and Cape Cods provide 600–900 sq ft of potential ADU space. Includes egress windows, separate entrance, kitchen, bathroom, fire separation, and COO from whichever authority governs your parcel — City, MoCo, or PGC.

Typical Cost: $58,000 – $112,000
Split-Level Advantage

Split-Level Lower-Level ADU

West Laurel and Montpelier — both in unincorporated Montgomery County — have a significant concentration of 1960s–80s split-level homes. The partially above-grade lower level offers natural light from side windows, ceiling heights of 7'4"–8'2", and often an existing side entrance — making split-level ADU conversions the fastest and most cost-effective path for these properties. Many also have existing lower-level plumbing rough-ins that reduce bathroom addition costs. DMV-GC Group checks for rough-ins during every West Laurel and Montpelier consultation.

Typical Cost: $55,000 – $105,000
Where Garage Exists

Garage Conversion ADU

Where a detached or attached garage exists — common in older City of Laurel neighborhoods and some Montpelier properties — conversion to an ADU is often a fast, cost-effective path. Includes insulation, HVAC, plumbing, egress, interior finishes, kitchen, and bathroom — all with the correct jurisdiction's permits and Certificate of Occupancy. Feasibility depends on garage size, ceiling height, and utility connection distance from the main house.

Typical Cost: $55,000 – $105,000
For Larger Lots

Attached In-Law Suite Addition

A new addition with separate entrance, bedroom, bathroom, and kitchen. Most feasible on larger parcels in Laurel Pines, Montpelier, and some West Laurel areas where rear yard depth is sufficient after setbacks. PG County and MoCo setback requirements vary — DMV-GC Group confirms rear yard feasibility for your specific parcel's jurisdiction during the free consultation before any addition is designed.

Typical Cost: $92,000 – $190,000
