Cabin John Fence Permits, Property Lines & HOA Requirements

Fence projects in Cabin John are subject to Montgomery County requirements because Cabin John is an unincorporated community within Montgomery County. Montgomery County Department of Permitting Services identifies residential fence permits as part of its permitting process, and county guidance states that a new fence requires a fence permit. The county also requires the fence location to be shown on the site plan and states that the fence must be wholly on the property rather than placed directly on the property line.
