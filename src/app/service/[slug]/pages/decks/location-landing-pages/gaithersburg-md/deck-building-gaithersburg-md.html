Gaithersburg Has Two Permit Authorities — and Many Neighborhoods Also Require HOA Approval Before Permits Are Filed

Gaithersburg is a mosaic of permit jurisdictions and HOA governance structures that most deck contractors in the area do not fully understand. Properties within the incorporated City of Gaithersburg — including Kentlands and Lakelands — file deck permits with the City's own Department of Public Works. Properties in unincorporated portions of the Gaithersburg area — including Crown Farm — file with Montgomery County DPS. Then, independently of which government issues the permit, many Gaithersburg communities also require HOA Architectural Review Committee approval before any permit is filed — Kentlands, Lakelands, Crown Farm, and Washingtonian Woods all have active ARC processes. Filing with the wrong permit authority, or skipping HOA approval, can invalidate the entire permit and result in required removal. DMV-GC Group verifies jurisdiction and HOA requirements for every Gaithersburg address before any work begins.

City of Gaithersburg vs Montgomery County — Which Permit Authority Governs Your Deck

This is the most important practical question for any Gaithersburg deck project. The answer depends on your specific parcel's location — not your mailing address, and not proximity to a neighbor who filed with one authority or the other.

🏛️ City of Gaithersburg

City DPW Permits
  • Permit authority: City of Gaithersburg Dept. of Public Works
  • Covers properties within the incorporated city limits
  • Kentlands: City permits
  • Lakelands: City permits
  • Historic city center neighborhoods: City permits
  • City has own plan review staff and inspection schedule
  • City permits required separately from any HOA ARC approval
  • COO issued by City after final inspection

📋 Montgomery County

MoCo DPS Permits
  • Permit authority: Montgomery County DPS
  • Covers unincorporated MoCo portions of Gaithersburg area
  • Crown Farm: MoCo permits (outside city limits)
  • Some Washingtonian Woods addresses: verify by parcel
  • Neighborhoods along I-270 corridor outside city: MoCo
  • Same MoCo permit process as Rockville, North Bethesda
  • MoCo COO required before deck occupancy
⚠️ Critical for Gaithersburg: Kentlands and Lakelands are within the City of Gaithersburg — they require City DPW permits, not Montgomery County DPS permits. Crown Farm is outside the city limits — it requires Montgomery County DPS permits. Many Gaithersburg contractors file the wrong permit because they don't verify parcel-level jurisdiction. DMV-GC Group verifies the correct authority for every Gaithersburg address using parcel boundary data before any application is filed.

This is a separate process from the building permit and must typically be completed before the permit is filed. DMV-GC Group manages both processes simultaneously wherever the HOA allows.

📋 What DMV-GC Group Prepares for Gaithersburg HOA ARC Submissions

  • Site plan: Survey-based plot plan showing the home footprint, property lines, existing structures, and proposed deck location with dimensions — required by all four Gaithersburg HOAs for deck ARC submissions.
  • Elevation drawings: Front, side, and rear elevation drawings showing the deck's height relative to the home and ground, railing style, stair location, and visual relationship to the home's existing exterior. Prepared to ARC submission standards.
  • Material and color specifications: Manufacturer name, product line, SKU, and color swatch for all deck surface, railing, and post materials — required by Kentlands and Lakelands ARC to verify compatibility with community design standards.
  • Parallel permit preparation: While ARC review is underway, DMV-GC Group prepares the City or County building permit application — so that the permit can be filed the same day ARC approval is received, minimizing total pre-construction timeline.
  • ARC revision management: If the ARC requests modifications to the design, DMV-GC Group prepares and resubmits the revised documentation — no additional charge for first-round revision responses.
