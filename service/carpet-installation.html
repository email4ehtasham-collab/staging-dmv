Carpet Styles We Install in Bethesda, MD

The right carpet style affects how your room looks, feels underfoot, and holds up over time. Here are the main carpet styles we install for Bethesda homeowners — along with where each performs best:

Most Popular

Plush / Cut Pile Carpet

Soft, dense, and luxurious underfoot — cut pile is the most popular carpet style for Bethesda bedrooms and formal living spaces. The evenly cut fibers create a smooth, velvet-like surface that feels exceptional underfoot and looks elegant in any room.

Best for: Master bedrooms, guest rooms, formal living rooms
Most Durable

Berber / Loop Pile Carpet

Berber carpet uses uncut, looped fibers that are extremely durable and resistant to matting and crushing. Its low-profile, casual texture hides footprints and vacuum marks well — ideal for high-traffic areas. Available in flecked, multi-tone patterns that disguise everyday dirt and wear.

Best for: Basements, hallways, family rooms, offices
Casual & Cozy

Frieze / Twisted Pile Carpet

Frieze carpet features tightly twisted, curled fibers that give it a textured, casual appearance. The twisted construction helps the carpet resist crushing and matting — making it one of the most durable cut-pile styles available. Popular in Bethesda family rooms and kids' rooms.

Best for: Family rooms, kids' bedrooms, playrooms, hallways
Luxury Choice

Patterned / Cut-and-Loop Carpet

Cut-and-loop carpet combines cut and looped fibers to create geometric and sculptural patterns. The pattern variation also helps hide stains and footprints. A popular choice for Bethesda homeowners who want a more decorative carpet that adds visual interest to a room.

Best for: Dining rooms, formal areas, statement bedrooms
Stair Specialist

Stair Carpet (Waterfall & Wrapped)

Stair carpet installation requires specific techniques to ensure safety, durability, and a clean finish. We install both waterfall (carpet flows over the nose of each tread) and wrapped (carpet wraps around each tread and riser) styles — selecting the right pad thickness to prevent premature wear at the stair edge.

Best for: All stair types — straight, L-shaped, curved
Below Grade

Basement Carpet & Carpet Tile

Basements require moisture-aware carpet selection. We install moisture-resistant carpet over waterproof pad for finished basements in Bethesda — and recommend carpet tiles for areas with any history of water intrusion, since individual tiles can be replaced if moisture damage occurs.

Best for: Finished basements, media rooms, basement bedrooms

Carpet Padding — Why It Matters More Than Most Homeowners Realize

The carpet pad underneath your carpet is invisible — but it has an enormous impact on how your carpet feels, performs, and how long it lasts. Skimping on padding is one of the most common mistakes homeowners make when replacing carpet in Bethesda. Here's what to know:

🛋️

Rebond Foam Pad

The most common residential pad. A minimum 6–8 lb density at 7/16" thickness is recommended for Bethesda bedrooms and living areas. Higher density = longer carpet life.

☁️

Memory Foam Pad

Provides the softest, most luxurious underfoot feel. Popular in master bedrooms. Slightly more expensive than rebond but dramatically improves comfort.

🏗️

Fiber / Felt Pad

Dense, flat pad ideal for Berber loop carpet and commercial-style installations. Prevents the loops from snagging on a soft pad surface. Required for most Berber warranties.

💧

Moisture-Barrier Pad

For basements and pet-friendly homes — a plastic moisture barrier on the top surface prevents liquids from soaking through to the subfloor. Essential for below-grade carpet installations.

🪜

Stair Pad

Thinner (3/8"), denser pad specifically for stairs. Prevents the carpet from "bottoming out" on the stair nose with every step — which causes premature wear at the edge.

🔇

Acoustic / Sound-Reduction Pad

For multi-story Bethesda homes — acoustic carpet padding significantly reduces impact sound transmission between floors. Popular in master bedrooms above living spaces.

💡 DMV General Contracting Group Padding Policy: We never install below-minimum padding to cut costs. Every carpet installation in Bethesda gets the right pad for the room, the carpet style, and how the space will be used. A better pad protects your carpet investment — and most carpet manufacturers require a minimum pad specification to honor their warranty.

Our Carpet Installation Process in Bethesda

Here is exactly how DMV General Contracting Group handles your carpet installation in Bethesda — from free estimate to finished room:

1

Free In-Home Estimate & Room Measurement

We visit your Bethesda home, measure all rooms accurately (including closets and alcoves), assess your subfloor condition, and walk you through carpet styles, fiber options, and padding grades — at no charge. We bring samples so you can see colors and textures in your home's actual lighting.

2

Carpet & Padding Selection

We help you choose the right carpet style, fiber, pile height, and color for each room — along with the appropriate padding grade. We provide a detailed written quote showing carpet cost, padding cost, and labor separately so you understand exactly what you're paying for.

3

Old Carpet Removal & Disposal

On installation day, we remove all existing carpet and padding, pull up tack strips if they need to be replaced, and dispose of all old materials. Old carpet removal and disposal are included at no extra charge when you install new carpet with DMV General Contracting Group.

4

Subfloor Inspection & Preparation

We inspect the bare subfloor for squeaks, soft spots, raised nail heads, and uneven areas. Any issues are addressed before the pad goes down — a smooth, solid subfloor is essential for a carpet that stays wrinkle-free and performs well long-term.

5

Padding & Tack Strip Installation

New tack strips are installed around the room perimeter. Padding is cut to fit precisely within the tack strips and secured flat to the subfloor — no overlaps, no gaps, no bubbles. The right pad installation is what gives carpet its smooth, even feel from wall to wall.

6

Carpet Installation, Seaming & Stretching

We lay the carpet with seams planned to be as invisible as possible — placed in low-traffic areas and following the room's natural light direction. Carpet is power-stretched to the tack strips to eliminate any future buckling. Seams are heat-bonded and hand-blended for the cleanest possible finish.

7

Transitions, Trim & Final Walkthrough

All transition strips and door thresholds between carpet and hard flooring are installed cleanly. We trim any excess carpet at doorways and closet entries, vacuum the new carpet, and do a final walkthrough with you — reviewing every seam and edge before we leave.

Carpet vs. Hardwood — Making the Right Choice for Each Room

A common question among Bethesda homeowners planning a renovation is whether to carpet or hardwood a particular room. Here's the honest answer — and it's not one-size-fits-all:

Carpet wins in bedrooms for comfort, warmth, acoustic softness (great for sleeping), and cost. There is simply nothing as comfortable underfoot in the morning as quality plush carpet. Carpet also wins in basements for the same reasons — it transforms a cold, hard space into a warm, livable room.

Hardwood wins in main living areas — living rooms, dining rooms, hallways, and kitchens — for resale value, durability, and ease of cleaning. Bethesda buyers consistently prioritize hardwood in these spaces, and it adds more measurable home value than carpet.

The smartest approach for most Bethesda homes: hardwood or LVP in all main living areas and hallways, carpet in all bedrooms and the finished basement. DMV General Contracting Group handles both — and can coordinate a whole-home flooring project that includes both carpet and hardwood in the same timeline.
