R-200 Zoning & Permitting for Potomac Home Additions

📋 Potomac Permit Process — What Every Addition Requires

  • R-200 zoning setback review — first step before any design: Potomac is predominantly zoned R-200 — requiring minimum lot size of approximately 2 acres, front setback of 50 feet, side setbacks of 25 feet per side, and rear setback of 35 feet. These setbacks define the maximum buildable envelope within which the addition must fit. DMV General Contracting Group performs this review at the free consultation.
  • HOA Architectural Review Committee (ARC) — required before MoCo DPS permit in many Potomac communities: Many Potomac neighborhoods require HOA ARC approval for any exterior modification including additions, before the Montgomery County DPS permit can be filed. DMV General Contracting Group prepares and manages the full HOA ARC submission in-house, including attending ARC meetings where required.
  • Montgomery County DPS building permit — filed at 2425 Reedie Drive, Wheaton: All Potomac building permits are filed with Montgomery County DPS. Potomac is unincorporated Montgomery County with no separate municipal permit office. MoCo DPS addition permit review typically takes 8–12 weeks for Potomac estate-scale projects.
  • Structural engineering — required for all load-bearing additions: All Potomac additions involving load-bearing wall removal, second-story additions, or large room additions require stamped structural engineering documents as part of the MoCo DPS permit package. DMV General Contracting Group's in-house structural engineers provide all stamped drawings.
  • Trade permits — electrical, plumbing, HVAC: Each trade permit is pulled separately from MoCo DPS and requires its own rough-in inspection before walls close. DMV General Contracting Group's in-house licensed electricians, plumbers, and HVAC technicians pull and manage their own trade permits.

Frequently Asked Questions — Home Additions in Potomac, MD

What zoning applies to home additions in Potomac, MD?
Most of Potomac is zoned R-200 — a low-density estate residential classification requiring approximately 2-acre minimum lot size and setting minimum setbacks of 50 feet front, 25 feet per side, and 35 feet rear. R-200 setbacks define the buildable envelope within which any addition must fit. On most Potomac estate lots, these setbacks leave substantial buildable area for rear and side additions. DMV General Contracting Group performs a full R-200 setback and FAR review at the free consultation — at no charge — to confirm what is buildable on your specific parcel before design begins.
Do I need HOA approval for a home addition in Potomac?
Many Potomac communities require HOA Architectural Review Committee (ARC) approval for additions and exterior modifications — including rear additions not visible from the street. The ARC submission typically includes architectural drawings, exterior elevations, material specifications, and a site plan. ARC approval is typically required before MoCo DPS permit filing. DMV General Contracting Group prepares and manages the complete HOA ARC submission in-house, initiating the process in parallel with permit document preparation so HOA and county review run concurrently rather than sequentially.
Where are Potomac home addition permits filed?
Potomac is unincorporated Montgomery County — all home addition permits are filed with Montgomery County DPS at 2425 Reedie Drive, Wheaton, MD 20902. Unlike Kensington, Garrett Park, Gaithersburg, or Rockville, Potomac has no separate municipal permit office. MoCo DPS handles all structural, electrical, plumbing, and HVAC permits for Potomac additions. DMV General Contracting Group's in-house permit team manages the full submission and all county correspondence — you do not contact the county at any point in the process.
