Split-Level Structural Expertise — Why It Matters for Your Silver Spring, MD Addition

Silver Spring's 1950s–1970s split-levels and colonials present structural conditions that differ meaningfully from newer construction — load-bearing walls at unexpected locations, multi-level floor plates that complicate addition connections, and original electrical panels that often need upgrading when an addition expands the home's load. DMV General Contracting Group's in-house structural engineers and architects are experienced designing additions that integrate correctly with Silver Spring's split-level architecture — not just attaching a room to the rear elevation without understanding the structural context.

Our Home Addition Process in Silver Spring, MD

1

Free In-Home Consultation & Feasibility Review

We visit your Silver Spring, MD home, confirm the correct permit jurisdiction, review zoning and setback requirements, assess the existing structure — with particular attention to split-level structural conditions — and discuss which addition types are feasible — at no cost. Realistic budget range provided before any design work begins.

2

Architectural Design & 3D Renderings

In-house architects develop concept plans, floor layouts, and 3D renderings refined until they reflect your vision — reviewed by our construction team to ensure buildability within budget.

3

Structural Engineering & Permit Submission

In-house structural engineers perform load calculations and specify all structural elements — with detailed attention to Silver Spring's split-level and colonial structural conditions. Complete permit package submitted to MoCo DPS. All permit correspondence managed in-house.

4

Foundation, Framing & Exterior Construction

Site preparation, footings, foundation, and structural framing completed by our licensed crews. Exterior roofing, siding, and windows installed to match your home's existing exterior materials and profile.

5

Mechanical Rough-In & Interior Finishes

Licensed electricians, plumbers, and HVAC technicians extend all systems into the addition — including electrical panel upgrades when the addition expands the home's load. Interior finishes installed — matching or upgrading your home's existing standard. All permit inspections managed in-house.

6

Final Inspections, Punch List & Walkthrough

All required final inspections scheduled and managed. Thorough punch list walkthrough before project close. Written warranty documentation provided.
