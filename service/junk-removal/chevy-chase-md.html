What We Accept — and What We Don't

✅ We Remove

  • Furniture — sofas, beds, tables, chairs, dressers
  • Appliances — refrigerators, washers, dryers, ovens
  • Construction debris — drywall, lumber, tile, flooring
  • Boxes, bags, and general household junk
  • Garage contents — tools, sports equipment, stored items
  • Basement and attic cleanout items
  • Yard waste and outdoor furniture
  • Electronics (e-waste disposal at appropriate facility)
  • Mattresses and box springs
  • Exercise equipment

❌ We Do Not Remove

  • Hazardous waste (chemicals, solvents, paint cans with liquid)
  • Asbestos-containing materials (requires certified abatement)
  • Medical waste or biohazard materials
  • Propane tanks (full or partially full)
  • Automotive fluids or oil
  • Lead paint chips (requires certified handling)
  • Explosive or flammable materials

If your Chevy Chase property has any of these materials, we can refer you to appropriate licensed specialists for safe handling and disposal.
