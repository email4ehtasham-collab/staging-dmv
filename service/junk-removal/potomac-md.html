What We Accept — and What We Don't

✅ We Remove

  • Furniture — full estate suites, oversized sofas, antiques
  • Appliances — refrigerators, ovens, wine coolers, washers, dryers
  • Construction debris — drywall, lumber, tile, stone, flooring
  • Hardscape debris — old concrete, pavers, stone, pool coping
  • Garage contents — tools, sports equipment, stored items
  • Basement and attic contents — boxes, furniture, stored goods
  • Outdoor furniture and patio items
  • Electronics (e-waste at appropriate facility)
  • Mattresses, box springs, and bedding
  • Exercise and sporting equipment

❌ We Do Not Remove

  • Hazardous waste — chemicals, solvents, liquid paint
  • Asbestos-containing materials (requires certified abatement)
  • Medical or biohazard waste
  • Propane tanks (full or partially full)
  • Automotive fluids or oil
  • Lead paint chips (requires certified handling)
  • Explosive or flammable materials

If your Potomac property has any of these materials, we can refer you to appropriate licensed specialists for safe handling and disposal.
