Concrete Block Foundations in Wheaton — What ADU Buyers Need to Know

Wheaton's 1950s–1960s construction boom produced a significant number of homes with concrete block foundation walls — a construction method common in postwar Maryland but less prevalent in newer construction. For ADU purposes, concrete block foundations require specific moisture management that differs from poured concrete.

🧱 Concrete Block Foundation ADUs — DMV-GC Group's Assessment Approach

  • Why block is different: Concrete block walls are inherently more porous than poured concrete — moisture migrates through the mortar joints and block faces rather than through cracks as in poured concrete. This means interior moisture management must address the entire wall surface, not just specific crack locations.
  • Assessment first: DMV-GC Group evaluates every Wheaton block foundation basement for moisture infiltration signs — efflorescence (white mineral deposits on block faces), mortar joint deterioration, and staining at the floor-wall joint — before any ADU design is finalized. An ADU built over an unaddressed block foundation moisture problem will develop tenant complaints and damage within 2–3 years.
  • Interior waterproofing approach: The most reliable moisture management for Wheaton block foundations is interior waterproofing — a drainage mat system on the interior of the block wall directing any moisture to a perimeter drain channel that routes to a sump pit. This approach manages moisture permanently without requiring exterior excavation in Wheaton's typically developed rear yards.
  • Framing strategy: Block foundation walls must be furred out with a pressure-treated framing system before drywall is applied — the framing creates an air gap between the block and the finish wall that prevents moisture from wicking directly into the drywall. DMV-GC Group uses pressure-treated bottom plates on all Wheaton block foundation ADU projects as a standard practice.
  • Sump pump specification: Montgomery County's clay soils and Wheaton's terrain create groundwater conditions that require properly sized sump pumps — with battery backup — for any finished basement ADU. Battery backup is non-negotiable for a rented ADU where the homeowner may not be present during a power outage storm event.
  • Alternative: split-level ADU: Where a Wheaton home has both a split-level lower level and a full below-grade basement, the split-level lower level is often the more straightforward ADU choice — the partially above-grade walls are less prone to block moisture infiltration, and the existing light and entrance reduce conversion costs. DMV-GC Group assesses both options and recommends the most practical path for your specific home.

Montgomery County ADU Requirements for Wheaton Homeowners

Wheaton is in unincorporated Montgomery County — all ADU permits go through Montgomery County DPS, and the county's ADU ordinance governs all zoning requirements. Key rules for Wheaton:

📋 Montgomery County ADU Ordinance — Key Rules for Wheaton

  • Lot size minimum: 6,000 sq ft. Most Wheaton single-family properties qualify, though some lots in denser parts of the community are at or near this threshold. DMV-GC Group verifies lot size during the consultation.
  • ADU size maximum: 1,200 sq ft or 50% of the main home's floor area, whichever is less. For Wheaton's typical 1,100–1,800 sq ft postwar homes, the 50% rule is often binding — a 1,200 sq ft split-level cannot have an ADU larger than 600 sq ft.
  • Egress in sleeping areas: Every bedroom in the ADU requires egress window compliance. Split-level above-grade windows may already satisfy this — below-grade windows typically require installation or enlargement.
  • Separate entrance: Required for all ADU types. Many Wheaton split-levels have this already. Below-grade basement ADUs typically require a new side or rear entrance cut.
  • Owner-occupancy: The property owner must reside in either the main home or the ADU. Pure investment property ADUs are not permitted under the current county ordinance.
  • Certificate of Occupancy: Required before the ADU can legally be rented. DMV-GC Group manages the COO process — all inspections and sign-offs — as part of every Wheaton ADU project.
