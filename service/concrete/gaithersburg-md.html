Concrete Finish Options for Gaithersburg Homeowners

🧹 Broom Finish

A practical textured finish offering slip resistance and a clean appearance. A strong choice for driveways and walkways.

🪨 Exposed Aggregate

Reveals decorative stone aggregate for added texture and a natural appearance, especially useful around pools and slopes.

🎨 Stamped Concrete

Imprints patterns such as slate, cobblestone, brick and wood while allowing integral color for a decorative finish.

🖌️ Colored / Integral Color

Pigment is mixed into the concrete for consistent color throughout the slab and can be combined with stamping.

✨ Salt Finish

A subtle textured surface created by pressing rock salt into wet concrete and removing it after curing.

🔲 Smooth / Trowel Finish

A hard smooth finish commonly used for garage floors and interior slabs where an ultra-smooth surface is appropriate.

What Makes a Quality Concrete Installation?

🔍 Proper Sub-Base Preparation

Unsuitable material is removed and the subgrade is prepared with a properly compacted aggregate base appropriate to the project.

💪 Correct Mix & Reinforcement

Concrete strength, air entrainment and reinforcement are selected for the intended use and Maryland exterior conditions.

💧 Drainage & Control Joints

Grades and control-joint locations are planned to direct water away from structures and help manage shrinkage cracking.

🛡️ Proper Curing & Sealing

Appropriate curing time and exterior sealing help protect the finished concrete from moisture, staining and freeze-thaw deterioration.

Our Concrete Installation Process in Gaithersburg, MD

1

Free On-Site Estimate & Assessment

We inspect the property, measure the work area, review existing concrete and discuss finish and design options.

2

Utility, Site & Permit Review

We review site conditions, applicable permitting and utility-marking requirements before excavation.

3

Excavation & Removal

Existing concrete is removed when required, unsuitable soil is addressed and the excavation is prepared for the new base.

4

Sub-Base Installation & Compaction

A properly graded aggregate base is installed and compacted in lifts for uniform support.

5

Formwork & Reinforcement

Forms are set to grade, drainage slopes are established, and rebar or mesh is positioned as required.

6

Concrete Pour & Finishing

Concrete is placed, struck off, floated and finished to the selected broom, aggregate, stamped, salt or smooth texture.

7

Curing, Sealing & Cleanup

We allow appropriate cure time, remove forms and debris, and complete sealing when the concrete reaches the recommended cure stage.

Concrete Repair & Resurfacing in Gaithersburg, MD

Not every damaged slab needs complete replacement. We assess cracks, spalling, surface scaling and settlement to determine whether repair, resurfacing or replacement makes the most sense.

Repair

Appropriate for isolated cracks, localized spalling and some surface deterioration when the underlying slab remains structurally sound.

Resurfacing

A cost-effective option for structurally sound concrete with widespread surface wear that needs a refreshed appearance.

Replacement

Recommended when the slab has major structural cracking, severe settlement, heaving, failed sub-base conditions or inadequate original construction.
