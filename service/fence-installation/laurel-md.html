Laurel Fence Permits, Property Lines & HOA Requirements

Fence projects in Laurel are subject to Montgomery County requirements because Laurel is an unincorporated community within Montgomery County. Montgomery County Department of Permitting Services identifies residential fence permits as part of its permitting process, and county guidance states that a new fence requires a fence permit. The county also requires the fence location to be shown on the site plan and states that the fence must be wholly on the property rather than placed directly on the property line.

Our Fence Installation Process in Laurel

1

Free On-Site Estimate & Property Walk

We visit your Laurel property, walk the proposed fence line, discuss privacy, security, pet, pool or aesthetic goals, review terrain and access, and prepare a detailed project estimate.

2

Material Selection & Project Review

We help select the fence material, height, style, color and gate configuration. Where applicable, HOA requirements and Montgomery County permitting considerations are reviewed before installation is scheduled.

3

Property Line & Utility Planning

Fence placement is reviewed against available property information, and underground utility locations are coordinated before digging. If property corners are unclear, professional survey verification may be recommended.

4

Post Setting & Concrete Anchoring

Posts are installed at appropriate spacing and depth for the selected fence system and site conditions, then checked for alignment and plumb before the concrete sets.

5

Panel, Rail & Picket Installation

Fence panels, rails, pickets or ornamental sections are installed true and level. On sloped properties, the fence is stepped or raked according to the selected design.

6

Gate Installation & Hardware

Walk gates and drive gates are installed with properly sized hinges and latches. Pool gates receive the applicable self-closing and self-latching hardware required for the project.

7

Final Inspection & Cleanup

We inspect the completed fence line, verify gate operation, remove installation debris and walk the finished project with the homeowner before closing the job.
