Flooring Types We Install in Bethesda, MD

Choosing the right flooring type is one of the most impactful decisions you can make for your Bethesda home — it affects how your space looks, feels, and holds up over time. Here are the main flooring materials we install across Bethesda and Montgomery County:

Most Valuable

Hardwood Flooring

Solid or engineered hardwood is the gold standard for Bethesda homes. It adds timeless beauty, warmth, and measurable resale value. Available in oak, maple, walnut, hickory, and more — in dozens of stain colors and finishes. Solid hardwood can be refinished multiple times over its lifetime.

Lifespan: 50–100 yearsCost: $8–$15/sq ft installed
Most Popular Now

Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP)

The fastest-growing flooring choice in Bethesda. 100% waterproof, scratch-resistant, comfortable underfoot, and available in ultra-realistic wood and stone looks. Perfect for kitchens, bathrooms, basements, and homes with pets or children. Significantly less expensive than hardwood.

Lifespan: 20–30 yearsCost: $5–$10/sq ft installed
Best for Wet Areas

Porcelain & Ceramic Tile

The top choice for bathrooms, kitchens, mudrooms, and laundry rooms in Bethesda. Porcelain is harder, less porous, and more durable than ceramic — ideal for high-traffic areas. Available in wood-look, stone-look, and modern large-format designs up to 24"×48".

Lifespan: 50+ yearsCost: $8–$18/sq ft installed
Budget Friendly

Laminate Flooring

A cost-effective alternative to hardwood with a realistic wood appearance. Modern laminate is durable, easy to maintain, and available in a wide range of styles. Best for above-grade living areas, bedrooms, and home offices in Bethesda homes.

Lifespan: 15–25 yearsCost: $4–$8/sq ft installed
Bedroom Comfort

Carpet

Carpet remains a popular choice for Bethesda bedrooms and finished basements — soft underfoot, warm, and sound-absorbing. Available in a wide range of pile styles, colors, and fibers (nylon, polyester, wool). We install carpet with professional-grade pad for maximum comfort and longevity.

Lifespan: 10–15 yearsCost: $4–$10/sq ft installed
Luxury Option

Natural Stone (Marble & Travertine)

Marble, travertine, slate, and limestone tile for Bethesda homeowners who want the ultimate in luxury and uniqueness. Each piece is naturally one-of-a-kind. Most popular in master bathrooms, entryways, and high-end kitchen installations across Bethesda's upscale neighborhoods.

Hardwood Floor Refinishing in Bethesda, MD

If your Bethesda home has existing hardwood floors that look worn, faded, scratched, or dated, hardwood floor refinishing may be all you need — no full replacement required. DMV General Contracting Group offers professional hardwood refinishing throughout Bethesda and Montgomery County.

Our refinishing process includes thorough sanding to remove the old finish, stain, and surface scratches, followed by your choice of new stain color (from natural/clear to rich dark walnut) and multiple coats of fresh polyurethane or oil finish. The result is floors that look brand new at a fraction of the cost of replacement.

💰 Refinishing vs. Replacement: Hardwood refinishing in Bethesda typically costs $3–$6 per square foot — compared to $8–$15 per square foot for new hardwood installation. If your existing hardwood is structurally sound, refinishing is almost always the smarter financial choice.

Our Flooring Installation Process in Bethesda

Here's exactly how DMV General Contracting Group handles your flooring project in Bethesda — from first visit to final trim:

1

Free In-Home Consultation & Measurement

We visit your Bethesda home, measure all rooms, assess your existing subfloor condition, and walk through material options with you — in person, at no charge. We bring samples so you can see colors in your own lighting.

2

Material Selection & Detailed Estimate

We provide a detailed written estimate broken down by room and material. Once you approve, we order your materials — we work with trusted suppliers to source quality flooring at competitive prices.

3

Subfloor Preparation

Before any new flooring goes down, we remove old flooring, inspect and repair the subfloor, fill any gaps or soft spots, and ensure the surface is level. A properly prepared subfloor is the foundation of a long-lasting installation.

4

Professional Installation

Our experienced installers lay your flooring with precise cuts, tight seams, proper expansion gaps, and clean transitions between rooms and materials. We protect your walls, cabinets, and furniture throughout the process.

5

Trim, Transitions & Final Cleanup

We install all baseboards, quarter rounds, transition strips, and thresholds for a polished, finished look. We do a thorough cleanup of all debris and dust before our final walkthrough with you.
