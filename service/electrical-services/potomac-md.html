Panel & Service Upgrades in Potomac — Why 400A Is Often the Right Answer

Potomac estate homes regularly need 400A electrical service — not because of any deficiency in the existing panel, but because the home's electrical demands have grown beyond what 200A can support simultaneously. Here is what Potomac homes are running that drives 400A demand:

⚡ A typical Potomac estate electrical load profile: Two Level 2 EV chargers (50A each) + air source heat pump (40–60A) + pool pump (20–30A) + whole-home standby generator transfer switch + home theater and AV systems + electric range and wall ovens + primary suite bathroom radiant floor heat = a simultaneous load that can approach or exceed 200A service capacity. A 400A service upgrade is the permanent solution — two 200A panels fed from a 400A meter base — giving Potomac estate homes the headroom to run every system at full capacity without load conflicts or breaker trips.

🚨 Federal Pacific & Zinsco Panels in Potomac — Fire Hazard Warning

Many Potomac homes built between 1960 and 1985 still have Federal Pacific Electric (Stab-Lok) or Zinsco panels — both identified as fire hazards because their breakers frequently fail to trip during overloads, allowing circuits to remain energized past safe amperage levels. These panels are found throughout Potomac's estate neighborhoods — in homes that are otherwise well-maintained and updated — because the panels were installed during original construction and the issue only became widely known later. If your Potomac home has a Federal Pacific or Zinsco panel, replacement is strongly recommended regardless of panel age or apparent condition. DMV General Contracting Group identifies and replaces these panels regularly in Potomac — typically replacing them with a full 200A or 400A upgrade that adds capacity while eliminating the fire hazard. Insurance carriers in Maryland increasingly require documentation of panel replacement for homes with Federal Pacific or Zinsco panels before issuing or renewing coverage.

Electrical Warning Signs in Potomac Estate Homes

⚡

Breakers Tripping When Multiple Systems Run

EV charger, heat pump, or pool pump tripping breakers — panel is undersized for estate electrical load. 400A upgrade indicated.

💡

Lights Dimming When Appliances Start

Visible dimming when HVAC, EV charger, or range starts — voltage sag indicating inadequate service capacity for simultaneous loads.

🔥

Federal Pacific or Zinsco Panel

Fire hazard panels common in Potomac homes built 1960–1985. Breakers fail to trip on overload — replacement strongly recommended.

🔌

Warm Outlets or Discoloration

Warm, discolored, or sparking outlets require immediate inspection — never normal behavior; wiring or connection failure indicated.

🏠

Two-Prong Outlets in Older Wings

Ungrounded two-prong outlets in original home sections — older wiring without ground, safety issue, and limits use of modern devices.

👃

Burning Smell Near Panel or Outlets

Any burning or electrical smell near panel, outlets, or switches is a fire emergency warning — immediate professional assessment needed.
