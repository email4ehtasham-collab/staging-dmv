Gaithersburg Has Two Separate Permit Jurisdictions for Electrical Work — and Filing at the Wrong One Means No Permit Is Issued, No Inspection Is Scheduled, and the Work Is Legally Unpermitted Regardless of What the Contractor Tells You

Gaithersburg's residential communities are split between two permit jurisdictions that apply based on where within Gaithersburg your property sits — not on your mailing address or zip code. Kentlands and Lakelands, the planned new urbanist communities along Kentlands Boulevard, are within the incorporated City of Gaithersburg — their electrical permits are filed with the City of Gaithersburg Department of Public Works. Crown Farm, Washingtonian Woods, and communities outside the city limits are in unincorporated Montgomery County — their electrical permits are filed with MoCo DPS at 2425 Reedie Drive, Wheaton. A contractor who does not know this distinction — or who assumes all Gaithersburg properties file with the county — will file at the wrong office for Kentlands and Lakelands projects. The permit is not issued, the work proceeds without one, and the Gaithersburg homeowner is left with unpermitted electrical work that creates insurance and resale problems down the road. Gaithersburg's Two Housing Eras — Different Electrical Needs

🏘️ Kentlands, Lakelands & Planned Communities (1988–2010s) — Modern Stock, Modern Upgrades

  • Built with 200A service as standard — adequate baseline for most household loads
  • Most common upgrade: EV charger — dedicated 240V circuit; panel capacity usually supports one charger without upgrade
  • 400A upgrade when two EVs + heat pump + garage workshop push beyond 200A capacity
  • Smart home wiring — Lutron, Leviton, and home automation increasingly common in this community
  • Standby generator — popular in Kentlands; HOA ARC placement requirements confirmed before permit filing
  • Permit: City of Gaithersburg DPW — not MoCo DPS

🏚️ Older Gaithersburg Homes (Pre-1985) — Mid-Century Electrical Conditions

  • 100A panels common — undersized for today's EV chargers, heat pumps, and modern kitchens
  • Federal Pacific panels — found in Gaithersburg homes built or updated 1960–1982; fire hazard requiring replacement
  • Aluminum branch circuit wiring — homes built 1965–1973; assessed at every free estimate for this vintage
  • GFCI protection absent — kitchens, bathrooms, and garages in pre-1985 homes frequently lack required GFCI protection
  • Permit: MoCo DPS or City DPW — depends on location; confirmed by parcel

Standby Generator Installation in Gaithersburg — HOA Requirements in Kentlands

🏘️ Kentlands ARC & Generator Placement — Confirmed Before Any Permit Is Filed

The Kentlands Community Association's Architectural Review Committee governs exterior modifications in the community — and standby generator installation, which involves a permanent exterior concrete pad, visible equipment, and an audible engine, is subject to ARC review in many Kentlands situations. Typical ARC considerations include generator placement relative to property lines and neighboring homes, screening or enclosure requirements, and noise level compliance. DMV General Contracting Group confirms Kentlands ARC requirements for generator placement at the free consultation — designing the pad location and any required screening to satisfy ARC standards before any permit is filed with the City of Gaithersburg DPW. We have installed generators in Kentlands and are familiar with the community's generator review process. For Crown Farm and other MoCo DPS communities, we confirm HOA requirements at the consultation and manage any required submissions before filing with MoCo DPS.
