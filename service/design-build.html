What Is Design-Build — And Why Does It Matter for Bethesda Homeowners?

The design-build method is a project delivery approach where a single firm is responsible for both the design and construction of your renovation or addition. This is fundamentally different from the traditional approach where a homeowner hires an architect separately, receives finished plans, bids those plans out to multiple general contractors, and then manages the relationship between two separate firms.

For Bethesda homeowners — where renovation projects tend to be complex, high-value, and subject to Montgomery County's detailed permitting requirements — the design-build approach eliminates the coordination friction that causes most renovation projects to go over budget and behind schedule.

Our architects, engineers, permit specialists, and licensed tradesmen are all on staff — working together from day one on your Bethesda project.
