Tub-to-Shower Conversions, Walk-In Showers & Bathroom Updates

For Rockville homeowners whose bathrooms feel dated or cramped, three projects deliver the biggest impact: converting an unused tub into a spacious walk-in shower, fully modernizing an original or builder-grade bath, or adding a bathroom to a finished basement.

Tub-to-shower conversions span the whole city — from the small cast-iron tubs in Twinbrook's 1950s baths to the oversized garden and jetted tubs in King Farm and Fallsgrove primary suites. We remove the existing tub, rebuild the wet area with a proper bonded waterproofing system, and install a custom tile or low-maintenance walk-in shower — often with frameless glass, niches, and bench seating — reclaiming the footprint for a true spa shower.

Adding or reconfiguring a bathroom — most often a full or half bath in a finished basement, or reworking a primary suite — requires careful planning of plumbing rough-in, drainage, and ventilation. DMV-GC Group's team assesses how your home is built before designing the most cost-effective approach, then manages the full City of Rockville or Montgomery County DPS permit process and any HOA approvals.
