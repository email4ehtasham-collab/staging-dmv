Why Waterproofing Is the Most Important Step in Your Potomac Bathroom

The single most important quality indicator in a bathroom renovation is something you'll never see after the project is complete — the waterproofing.

⚠️ What to Watch For: The most common sign of a poorly waterproofed shower is grout cracking and tile loosening within 2–5 years of installation — caused by water getting behind the tile and expanding the substrate.
