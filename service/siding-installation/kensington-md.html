Signs Your Kensington Home Needs New Siding

🔧

Failing Original Aluminum

Oxidation, dents, or paint failure on the aluminum installed on Kensington's homes in the 1950s–60s — a material that skipped the bungalow era entirely.

🔍

Rot at Window Openings

Soft, crumbling wood at sills and jambs — active moisture damage in the sheathing behind the siding at Kensington's older openings.

Buckled Original Wood Siding

Warped, cupped, or cracked original clapboard compressed under decades of add-on layers.

💧

Interior Water Stains

Interior walls cold to the touch in winter — typical of Kensington's pre-war homes with zero original wall insulation.

💨

Gaps at Gable Transitions

Separated or open siding at steep gable and dormer transitions — the most common failure point on Kensington's character homes.
