Kitchen Remodeling for Wheaton's Most Common Home Types

Wheaton grew primarily between the 1940s and 1970s — producing a dense concentration of postwar homes that are now among the most sought-after renovation candidates in Montgomery County. Each home type has specific kitchen characteristics that require a different renovation approach. Here's how DMV-GC Group approaches the most common Wheaton home types:

🏠

Split-Level Homes

Wheaton's most common home type. Split-level kitchens sit on a middle floor between living and sleeping levels — often closed off and disconnected. We redesign the layout to improve sightlines and flow, often by opening the wall toward the lower living level.

🏡

Ranch Homes

Single-story ranches offer excellent open-concept conversion potential. Removing a non-load-bearing interior wall between the kitchen and living area creates a dramatic transformation — one of the highest-ROI renovations we complete in Wheaton.

🏘️

Cape Cod Homes

Wheaton's postwar Cape Cods typically have tight, galley-style kitchens. We specialize in expanding these kitchens by reconfiguring walls into adjacent dining rooms — creating the open-plan layouts today's buyers expect.

🏛️

Brick Colonials

Larger colonials in Wheaton's established areas offer full gut renovation opportunities. These kitchens benefit most from complete layout redesigns — new custom cabinetry, island additions, and premium countertops that match the home's character.

🏢

Townhomes

Newer townhomes near Wheaton Plaza and University Boulevard typically have L-shaped or galley kitchens on the main level. Upgrades here focus on custom cabinetry, countertop replacement, and finish packages that modernize the space efficiently.

🔨

Older Bi-Levels

Bi-level homes — common in Wheaton's Glenmont area — have kitchens that feel elevated and separate. We design renovation solutions that visually connect the kitchen to the rest of the home while maximizing the available footprint.
