Carpet Fibers for Gaithersburg's Planned Community Colonials

Most Durable
💪

Solution-Dyed Nylon

Our most frequent recommendation for Gaithersburg colonials — solution-dyed nylon handles the foot traffic of bedrooms, stair runs, and lower-level family rooms well, and holds color at the high-wear areas like stair nosings and bedroom doorways where cheaper fibers mat and fade first. Solution-dyed means color is embedded through the full fiber rather than coating the surface — it resists bleach spots, fading from cleaning products, and the color loss that appears in traffic lanes over time. Available in a wide range of colors suited to Kentlands and Crown Farm's traditional colonial interior aesthetics. The workhorse fiber for Gaithersburg family homes.

Lifespan: 15–25 yrsGaithersburg Cost: $4 – $8 / sq ft installed
Best for Families
🐾

SmartStrand Triexta

The top recommendation for Gaithersburg families with pets or young children — SmartStrand triexta has permanent, built-in stain resistance that does not diminish over time the way topical treatments on nylon or polyester do. Softer than nylon and significantly more forgiving of the food spills, pet accidents, and tracked-in grime that are part of everyday family life in Kentlands and Crown Farm homes. Triexta performs particularly well in Gaithersburg's lower-level family rooms and finished basements — the high-use casual spaces where stain resistance matters most. Durable enough for stair runs when paired with a quality pad.

Lifespan: 15–25 yrsGaithersburg Cost: $4.50 – $9 / sq ft installed
Premium Choice
🐑

Wool Carpet

The premium fiber for Gaithersburg homeowners who want the best appearance and underfoot feel in primary suites or formal living spaces. Wool holds color depth and resilience over decades of use — the natural fiber that synthetic alternatives replicate only partially. Particularly well suited to Kentlands and Crown Farm colonials with larger primary suites where the feel of the floor is genuinely noticed daily. Wool should be avoided in below-grade basement spaces where moisture exposure is a risk — for Gaithersburg's finished basements, nylon or triexta with moisture-barrier padding is the correct specification.

Lifespan: 20–30+ yrsGaithersburg Cost: $9 – $17+ / sq ft installed
Value Option
✅

Polyester & PET Carpet

Modern polyester and PET (recycled polyester) carpets offer good initial stain resistance and a wide color and texture range at a lower price than nylon or triexta — a practical choice for guest rooms, rental units, or lower-priority spaces where cost is the primary driver. Polyester is initially soft but tends to mat at high-traffic areas — stair runs and doorway entries — faster than nylon or triexta over time, making it less suitable for Gaithersburg's main bedroom and stair run scopes. We present polyester alongside nylon and triexta at every Gaithersburg estimate with honest long-term durability context.

Signs Your Gaithersburg Home Needs New Carpet

🔍

Matting at Doorways & Stairs

Permanently flattened carpet at bedroom entries and stair nosings — original builder carpet past its performance life.

🎨

Traffic Lane Fading

Visible color differences between high-traffic paths and undisturbed areas under furniture — surface-dyed fiber losing color.

🤧

Persistent Odor

Pet or musty odor that cleaning does not resolve — embedded in the pad below the carpet surface.

🌊

Basement Dampness or Mold

Musty smell or mold at basement carpet edges — standard pad transmitting moisture from below-grade slab.

🧹

Rippling or Loose Areas

Waves or slack areas in bedroom or stair carpet — stretched beyond proper tension, needs re-stretching or replacement.

🪡

Fraying at Transitions

Unraveling edges at transition strips or stair nosings — structural fiber failure beyond what cleaning addresses.

Our Carpet Installation Process in Gaithersburg

1

Free In-Home Estimate — Every Room Measured, No HOA Delay

We visit your Gaithersburg home and measure every room, stair run, and basement area individually. We note subfloor conditions, identify whether moisture-barrier padding is needed in basement spaces (it always is), and confirm the full scope and sequence of the project. You receive a room-by-room itemized estimate. No HOA submission required before scheduling — interior carpet projects in Kentlands, Crown Farm, and all Gaithersburg communities proceed directly from your estimate approval to installation scheduling.

2

Fiber & Color Selection — Any Color, No ARC Restrictions

We bring solution-dyed nylon, SmartStrand triexta, wool, and polyester samples to your Gaithersburg home — showing colors and textures in your actual lighting conditions in each room. We discuss fiber recommendations by room and use, with honest comparisons of durability, stain resistance, and maintenance across Gaithersburg's family home context. Any color and any fiber you select can be ordered and installed without HOA approval or review of any kind.

3

Old Carpet Removal & Subfloor Inspection

Existing carpet and pad removed from all project areas and disposed of. Subfloor inspected after removal — in Gaithersburg's modern planned community construction, subfloor is consistently standard plywood that rarely requires repair. Tack strips assessed and replaced where needed. For Gaithersburg's finished basements, the concrete slab surface is checked for cracks or moisture staining before moisture-barrier pad is installed.

4

Pad Installation — Moisture-Barrier Below Grade, Premium Pad Above

Premium carpet padding installed in all above-grade rooms and stair runs. Moisture-barrier padding installed in all below-grade spaces — finished basements and any lower-level rooms on concrete slab. New tack strips installed around all room perimeters. Moisture-barrier pad is sealed at the bottom to prevent ground moisture transmission from the below-grade slab — the single most important installation detail in Gaithersburg's finished basement carpet projects.

5

Carpet Installation — Rooms First, Stairs Last

Carpet installed in all bedroom and above-grade rooms first, then stair run from top to bottom, then basement and lower-level rooms. Seams placed away from high-traffic paths and planned at the estimate stage — not improvised on the day. All transitions, threshold strips, and stair nosings installed and secured. For open-plan Gaithersburg colonials with large great rooms or combined living-dining areas, seam placement is discussed and agreed at the estimate before material is cut.

6

Final Stretch, Trim & Walkthrough

All carpet power-stretched to proper tension across every room. All edges trimmed cleanly at baseboards. Transition strips secured at all hard-surface boundaries. Full walkthrough on every level — checking every seam, stair nosing, and transition strip — before project close. Written warranty documentation provided.

Frequently Asked Questions — Carpet Installation in Gaithersburg, MD

Does the Kentlands or Crown Farm HOA need to approve my carpet replacement?
No. The Kentlands ARC and Crown Farm HOA govern exterior modifications only — siding, windows, additions, and changes visible from the street. Interior carpet color, fiber, and style are entirely your choice with no HOA submission, no ARC review, and no approval timeline. You can select any carpet and schedule installation immediately after the estimate. DMV General Contracting Group confirms this for your specific community at the estimate if you want certainty, but the review process will not affect any interior carpet project in Kentlands, Lakelands, Crown Farm, or any other Gaithersburg community.
What subfloor conditions should I expect under old carpet in a Kentlands or Crown Farm home?
Gaithersburg's planned community homes — built 1989–2015 with modern construction — have standard plywood subfloor throughout. Unlike older MoCo homes where pulling up carpet can reveal wide-plank hardwood or balloon framing surprises, Kentlands and Crown Farm colonials have clean, consistent modern subfloor. Subfloor assessment is part of our standard removal process, but the conditions in Gaithersburg's planned communities are reliably predictable — fewer unknowns, faster installation, and no era-specific surprises under the carpet.
Why does my Gaithersburg finished basement need moisture-barrier carpet padding?
Gaithersburg's finished basements sit on below-grade concrete slabs that transmit ground moisture upward year-round, even in basements that appear completely dry. Standard carpet padding on a below-grade slab absorbs that moisture and creates mold conditions within 2–5 years in Maryland's climate. Moisture-barrier padding has a sealed bottom layer that blocks moisture transmission from the slab while providing normal padding cushion and insulation. DMV General Contracting Group installs moisture-barrier padding on every Gaithersburg basement carpet project as standard practice.
What is the best carpet fiber for a Gaithersburg family home?
Solution-dyed nylon is our top recommendation for bedrooms and stair runs — excellent durability, color retention, and performance under family home traffic. SmartStrand triexta is our preferred recommendation for households with pets or young children — the permanent built-in stain resistance pays off significantly in lower-level family rooms and finished basements. Wool is the premium choice for primary suites where feel matters most. We recommend fiber by room at every estimate based on how the space is actually used.
