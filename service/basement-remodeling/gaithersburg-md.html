City of Gaithersburg vs Montgomery County Permits — What Homeowners Need to Know

This is one of the most important — and most commonly mishandled — aspects of basement renovation in Gaithersburg. Unlike most of the communities DMV-GC Group serves, Gaithersburg is an incorporated city with its own Building Department. Properties within the incorporated city limits require permits from the City of Gaithersburg, not from Montgomery County.

📋 Gaithersburg Permit Jurisdiction — How It Works

  • City of Gaithersburg permit jurisdiction: Properties within the incorporated city limits — including Kentlands, Lakelands, most of central Gaithersburg, and areas along MD-355 — require permits from the City of Gaithersburg's Department of Public Works. The City has its own plan review process, fee schedule, and inspection staff separate from the county.
  • Montgomery County permit jurisdiction: Properties that use a "Gaithersburg, MD" mailing address but fall outside the incorporated city limits — including communities in unincorporated areas along I-270 and in outer Gaithersburg — require Montgomery County DPS permits, not city permits.
  • How the confusion happens: Many properties in unincorporated Montgomery County use Gaithersburg as their mailing city based on zip code (20877, 20878, 20879) even though they are not within the incorporated city. Filing with the wrong authority causes permit delays, fee complications, and failed inspections.
  • Crown Farm / Rio: The Crown and Rio developments are in unincorporated Montgomery County despite the Gaithersburg address — Montgomery County DPS permits apply here.
  • Montgomery Village: Montgomery Village uses a Gaithersburg mailing address but is an unincorporated community — Montgomery County DPS permits apply.
  • DMV-GC Group verifies the correct permit jurisdiction for every Gaithersburg address using parcel boundary data before filing any permit — this is standard practice on every project, not an optional step.
⚠️ Important: If a contractor files your Gaithersburg basement renovation permit with the wrong authority, the permit is invalid. The work may be flagged during inspection, requiring correction and re-permitting. DMV-GC Group's permit team prevents this by verifying jurisdiction before any filing. Ask any Gaithersburg contractor whether they verify city vs county jurisdiction — if they don't know what you're asking, that's a warning sign.

HOA Coordination for Kentlands, Lakelands & Crown Basement Projects

Kentlands, Lakelands, and Crown are award-winning planned communities that have active homeowner associations — and those HOAs have real authority over renovation projects, particularly anything that involves exterior modifications or construction activity that affects neighbors and common areas.

For most basement renovation projects, HOA coordination in these communities primarily involves contractor access documentation and work-hour compliance rather than design review — since basement finishing typically doesn't affect the exterior of the home. However, egress window installation (which does affect the exterior) may require HOA review or notification in some communities.

🏘️ What HOA Coordination Involves in Gaithersburg's Planned Communities

  • Certificate of Insurance (COI): Many Gaithersburg HOAs require contractors to provide a COI naming the HOA as an additional insured before work begins. DMV-GC Group prepares and submits this as standard for every HOA community project.
  • Contractor registration: Some communities require contractors to register with the HOA or property management company before beginning work — providing license numbers, insurance documentation, and scope of work.
  • Work hour restrictions: HOAs in Kentlands and Lakelands typically restrict construction activity to weekday daytime hours — generally 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM or 6:00 PM. DMV-GC Group schedules all trades within these windows.
  • Parking and material staging: Contractor vehicles and material deliveries must use designated areas — never blocking community roads, fire lanes, or neighbors' driveways. We coordinate all delivery and staging logistics with the HOA and our crews.
  • Egress window notification: Cutting a new opening in an exterior wall — even on the rear or side — may require HOA notification or approval in some Gaithersburg communities. DMV-GC Group determines the specific requirement for your community before any exterior work is planned.
  • Dumpster placement: Many HOAs have specific rules about dumpster placement — requiring placement in driveways, not on streets or in common areas. We coordinate this in advance to avoid violations and delays.
