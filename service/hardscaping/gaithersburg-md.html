Gaithersburg Hardscaping Expertise: Gaithersburg's planned communities — Kentlands, Lakelands, Crown Farm, and Washingtonian Woods — each have their own character, lot configurations, and HOA requirements. And critically, Gaithersburg has two separate permit jurisdictions: Kentlands and Lakelands properties file structural hardscaping permits with the City of Gaithersburg DPW, while most other Gaithersburg communities file with MoCo DPS. Filing at the wrong office means no permit is issued — and an unpermitted retaining wall or outdoor kitchen creates resale and insurance documentation problems. DMV General Contracting Group confirms the correct permit authority for every Gaithersburg address at the free consultation and manages all filings in-house.

Gaithersburg's Two Permit Jurisdictions — Confirmed Before Any Work Begins

🏛️ City of Gaithersburg DPW — Kentlands & Lakelands

  • Communities covered: Kentlands, Lakelands
  • Retaining walls over 30'', outdoor kitchens with gas or electrical, and driveway road connections filed with City of Gaithersburg DPW
  • Separate from MoCo DPS — county does not accept permit applications for City of Gaithersburg properties
  • Kentlands ARC approval also required for exterior modifications before City DPW permit is filed
  • DMV-GC files at City DPW and coordinates ARC submissions concurrently

🏛️ Montgomery County DPS — Crown Farm, Washingtonian Woods & Others

  • Communities covered: Crown Farm, Washingtonian Woods, Flower Hill, Montgomery Village, and most Gaithersburg communities outside city limits
  • All structural hardscaping permits filed with MoCo DPS at 2425 Reedie Drive, Wheaton
  • Separate from City of Gaithersburg DPW — city office does not cover these properties
  • HOA requirements vary by community — Crown Farm and Washingtonian Woods HOAs may have their own ARC processes
  • DMV-GC confirms jurisdiction by address and files at MoCo DPS for covered properties
Important — Gaithersburg mailing address alone does not determine which permit office applies. Two adjacent homes with identical Gaithersburg addresses may file at different offices depending on which community their parcel falls within. DMV General Contracting Group confirms the correct permit jurisdiction for every Gaithersburg hardscaping project at the free consultation — by parcel, not by neighborhood name or zip code.

Kentlands ARC — What Hardscaping Requires Approval

Kentlands Architectural Review Committee (ARC): Kentlands is a carefully planned new urbanist community where exterior modifications — including patio additions, retaining wall installations, driveway changes, and outdoor kitchen structures visible from the street or neighboring properties — typically require ARC approval before City of Gaithersburg DPW permits are issued. The ARC process requires submission of design plans, material specifications, and color documentation. DMV General Contracting Group prepares all required ARC documentation, submits on your behalf, and coordinates the ARC and City DPW permit processes concurrently so neither creates unnecessary project delay. Kentlands ARC-compatible materials (brick, natural stone, wood) are specified from the design stage — not selected and then changed after ARC rejection.

Hardscaping Materials by Gaithersburg Community

Gaithersburg's planned communities have distinct architectural characters — Kentlands' neo-traditional brick and shingle-style homes call for different hardscaping materials than Crown Farm's contemporary colonials or Washingtonian Woods' traditional profiles. DMV General Contracting Group recommends materials for each community with the architectural character and HOA specifications as the guiding framework.

🧱 Brick — Kentlands First Choice

Reclaimed or new brick in running bond, herringbone, or basket weave — the most period-appropriate and ARC-compatible hardscaping material for Kentlands' new urbanist character. References the community's brick-heavy streetscapes. Warm tone, durable, repairable. Most likely to pass Kentlands ARC review without revision.

🪨 Natural Bluestone — All Communities

Uniform-cut or irregular bluestone works across all Gaithersburg communities. Most popular upgrade material in Gaithersburg. ARC-compatible for Kentlands in traditional cut profiles. Contemporary profiles work for Crown Farm and Washingtonian Woods. Cool gray tone and natural texture complement both neo-traditional and contemporary architectural profiles.

🧱 Concrete Pavers — Practical Choice

Earth-tone tumbled or natural-finish pavers for all Gaithersburg communities. For Kentlands, smaller unit sizes in warm earth tones read as more period-compatible than large contemporary slabs. For Crown Farm and Washingtonian Woods, larger contemporary profiles are appropriate. HOA approved colors confirmed before ordering for all communities.

🪨 Travertine — Crown Farm & Washingtonian Woods

Warm cream and tan tones — popular for Crown Farm and Washingtonian Woods outdoor kitchen areas and contemporary home profiles. Less period-authentic than brick or bluestone for Kentlands' traditional character, but appropriate for newer Gaithersburg communities where contemporary materials are acceptable.

🧱 Large-Format Porcelain — Newer Homes Only

Appropriate for Crown Farm and Washingtonian Woods' newer contemporary homes. Not appropriate or ARC-compatible for Kentlands' traditional character. Non-porous, stain-resistant, and available in premium stone-look profiles. Frost-rated for Maryland outdoor use. HOA approved profiles confirmed before selection.

🏗️ Stamped Concrete — Older Neighborhoods

Most appropriate for older Gaithersburg neighborhoods where there is no HOA color restriction and budget is the primary driver. Cobblestone and slate patterns in earth tones. Not recommended for Kentlands or Crown Farm where HOA specifications apply. Sealed for Maryland's climate.
