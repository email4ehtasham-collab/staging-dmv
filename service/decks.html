Do You Need a Permit to Build a Deck in Bethesda, MD?

Yes — and this is one of the most important things to understand before starting a deck project in Bethesda. Montgomery County requires a building permit for decks that are:

  • Attached to the primary residence
  • More than 30 inches above grade at any point
  • 200 square feet or larger
  • Supporting any structure (pergola, roof, covered area)

Building a deck without the required permit in Montgomery County can result in fines, required demolition, and complications when selling your home. DMV General Contracting Group handles all required Montgomery County deck permits on every project — you don't need to visit any county office or manage any paperwork.

⚠️ Bethesda Homeowner Warning: Many homeowners have purchased Bethesda properties only to discover an unpermitted deck that must be demolished or brought up to code before closing. Always use a licensed, permit-pulling contractor for your deck build. DMV General Contracting Group handles every permit as a standard part of our process.

Deck Repair vs. Deck Replacement — Which Do You Need?

Many Bethesda homeowners come to us with an existing deck that needs attention. The question is whether to repair it or replace it entirely. Here's how we help you decide:

Repair makes sense when: the structural frame and posts are solid, only a few boards are rotting or damaged, the deck is less than 10 years old, and the repairs are cosmetic or limited in scope. Common repairs we handle include board replacement, railing replacement, stair repair, and ledger board reinforcement.

Replacement makes more sense when: the structural framing shows widespread rot or termite damage, the deck is more than 15–20 years old, the deck was built without permits (a common issue in Bethesda's older housing stock), or when you want to upgrade from wood to composite. In most cases, replacing an old failing deck costs less in the long run than repeated repairs — and gives you a brand-new, warrantied structure.

DMV General Contracting Group assesses every deck honestly during the free consultation — we'll tell you what's worth fixing and when a full replacement is the smarter investment.
