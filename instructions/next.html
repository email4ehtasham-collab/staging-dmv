General Contractor Value

Is It Worth Getting a General Contractor?

Yes, once a job spans several trades or needs a permit. A general contractor usually charges 10% to 20% of the project cost, and up to 25% or 30% on complex work. That fee covers hiring and scheduling the trades, pulling permits and standing behind the result. For a single-trade repair, such as a faucet or a wall patch, hiring that trade directly is usually cheaper.
