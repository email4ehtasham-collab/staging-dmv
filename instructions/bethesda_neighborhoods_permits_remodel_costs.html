Permits

Who Issues Building Permits in Bethesda?

Montgomery County's Department of Permitting Services (DPS) issues building permits in Bethesda, because Bethesda has no city government. Plumbing and gas permits come from WSSC Water. Under the DPS permit list, common Bethesda remodeling jobs break down this way:

Work Permit needed? Issued by
Addition or new deck Yes Montgomery County DPS
Interior alteration, such as moving walls, a kitchen or bath remodel or basement finishing Yes Montgomery County DPS
New or relocated plumbing and gas lines Yes WSSC Water
Electrical work Yes Montgomery County DPS
Window or door with a larger opening Yes Montgomery County DPS
Replacing cabinets, floor coverings, siding or roof covering Usually no Not required in most cases

Special Municipal & Historic Requirements

Homes inside incorporated towns such as Somerset or the Chevy Chase municipalities also need a town permit. Houses in a designated historic district need a Historic Area Work Permit before exterior changes. We check your address for both before design.
