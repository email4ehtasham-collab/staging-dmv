How Long Does a Kitchen Remodel Take?

Most kitchen remodels take 6 to 14 weeks of construction, after 4 to 8 weeks of design and permits. A refresh can finish in 2 to 4 weeks. A full gut with a new layout takes 10 to 14 weeks. This table splits a Maryland or DC kitchen schedule by phase:

Phase Duration & Details Schedule Range
Design and selections 2–4 weeks Weeks 0 – 4
Permit review 2–6 weeks; each jurisdiction sets its own review pace Weeks 2 – 8
Cabinet lead time 4–10 weeks, ordered during permit review Weeks 2 – 12
Construction: refresh 2–4 weeks 2–4 weeks build
Construction: mid-range remodel 6–10 weeks 6–10 weeks build
Construction: full gut with layout changes 10–14 weeks 10–14 weeks build
Tip

Temporary Kitchen Setup

The sink and range are out of use for most of the build. Set up a temporary kitchen in another room with the refrigerator, a microwave and a small table.
